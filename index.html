Privacy Policy — Liar's Game: The Room Beneath

Last updated: 10 November 2026 · Applies to version 1.0.0 and later

Liar's Game: The Room Beneath is a single-player game that runs on your device. This page explains, plainly, what the game does and does not do with your information. The game is published by Prince Bhalse, who is the data controller for this policy.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not create accounts, does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location or files, and does not send gameplay, device or personal data to us or to anyone else. There is no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind.

Network access

The app declares the Android internet permission for one reason only: Google Play Billing, which has to contact Google Play when you buy or restore the story unlock. The game itself never connects to a server of ours. Every part of the game, including all five episodes, plays with no connection once it is installed.

What is stored on your device

Your progress, settings, and the fact that you have seen the tutorial are written to the app's private storage as a single save file. That file stays on your device. It leaves only if you back it up with your own device backup tools, and uninstalling the game deletes it.

Purchases

If you buy the story unlock, the transaction is processed by Google Play. Google tells the app only whether the purchase exists, and the app confirms the purchase back to Google so it is not refunded. We receive only the anonymised sales reporting that Google provides to every developer. We never see your name, card details or payment address. Google's handling of that data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The game is a psychological mystery intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete. Deleting the app removes everything the game stored. Purchase records are held by Google Play and are subject to Google's policies.

Changes

If a future version adds crash reporting, analytics or any online feature, this policy will be updated before that version is published.
